## Welcome

I'm Pranav, a security engineer specializing in cloud security architecture, IAM policy enforcement, and distributed identity systems.

### What I Do

My engineering focus centers on:
- **Cloud Security Architecture**: Designing least-privilege identity structures and resilient guardrails across multi-cloud environments.
- **Identity & Access Management (IAM)**: Modernizing authentication, workload identity federation, and eliminating static credentials.
- **Zero-Trust Engineering**: Verifying explicitly, using least privileged access, and designing for breach assumption.
